---
name: Joventy WhatsApp pricing rules
description: Confirmed operational pricing and deposit rules for the Joventy WhatsApp agent.
---

The confirmed operational pricing is:

- Partial assistance: 600 USD total, with a 200 USD deposit.
- Full assistance: 1,500 USD total, with a 500 USD deposit and 1,000 USD balance.
- Normal appointment slot: 350 USD, generally paid after the appointment is obtained.
- Express appointment slot: 500 USD, generally paid after the appointment is obtained for destinations other than Spain.
- Spain normal slot: if the client already sent the embassy email, collect 150 USD deposit and 200 USD balance; if Joventy sends the email, no deposit and collect after obtaining the appointment.
- Spain express slot: if the client already sent the embassy email, collect 200 USD deposit and 300 USD balance; if Joventy sends the email, no deposit and collect 500 USD after obtaining the appointment.

**Why:** These rules were explicitly confirmed by the Joventy team and override older inconsistent wording in project content.

**How to apply:** The WhatsApp agent must qualify the requested service first, avoid presenting full-assistance pricing for slot-only questions, and always ask for the Spain email status before quoting the Spain deposit.
