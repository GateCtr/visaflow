If yourself, as applicant, are a family member of a European Union citizen, please bring to the Embassy/Consulate, at the moment you introduce your application, the following documentary evidence.

Documents regarding yourself as applicant:

  • Passport;
  • Marriage certificate (spouse of);
  • Birth certificate (child of);

    • Documents regarding European Union citizen:

  • Copy of the identity card or national passport;
  • Documents evidencing the status of stay in Belgium (copy of the application for a declaration of inscription (annex 19), of the declaration of inscription (annex 8), of the E-card or of a document evidencing the durability of stay (annex 8bis or E+-card));
  • Documents evidencing that the European Union citizen has the intention to stay during more than 3 months in Belgium (declaration of presence (annex 3ter), work contract, renting contract, registration with a school/university organized, recognized or funded by the authority, …)
  • Documents evidencing that the European Union citizen has the intention to stay during more than 3 months in Belgium (declaration of presence (annex 3ter), work contract, renting contract, registration with a school/university organized, recognized or funded by the authority, …)

    • The above list is not complete (not exhaustive).

    The Embassy or Consulate reserves the right to require supplementary documents if it is deemed necessary.

    N.B.: under European Union citizen it is meant an EU, EEA or CH citizen or a UK national who is a beneficiary of the EU-UK Withdrawal Agreement.
